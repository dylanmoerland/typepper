typepper — a spicy little typing game

A typing game and typing test where your speed is heat: type fast and clean to climb the Scoville scale from bell pepper to Carolina reaper, with combos, streaks and fever. Modes: sprint (10, 25 or 50 words), blitz (15, 30 or 60 seconds), zen (endless, no clock) and survival (a fuel clock that drains faster every level). Personal bests are saved.

Play it here in your browser, or install it in your terminal on macOS, Linux and Windows. Free and open source under the GPL.
